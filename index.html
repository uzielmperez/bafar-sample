Who we are

One of Mexico's leading food companies — integrated end to end.

From industrialization to distribution and commercialization, Grupo Bafar has a solid presence in national and international markets, backed by more than 35 years of experience.

Our growth comes from quality brands, great-tasting products with high nutritional value, coverage of the entire socioeconomic pyramid — and, above all, the work of our people.
